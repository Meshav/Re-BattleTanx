# 04 — Single-Player Campaign

## 4.1 Structure
Two acts mirroring the two original games, written with original characters.

- **Act I — "The Long Road West"** (BattleTanx homage): 12 missions, coast-to-coast US. Goal: rescue the protagonist's partner from a rival QueenLord.
- **Act II — "Global Front"** (Global Assault homage): 12 missions, US → Europe. Goal: stop an antagonist QueenLord hunting the protagonists' Edge-gifted child.
- **Secret missions**: 1 per act, unlocked by finding all hidden caches in that act.

~24 missions × 5–10 min ≈ 3–4 hours. Longer and more varied than the originals (a common criticism).

## 4.2 Economy: Tank Bucks (from Global Assault)
- Start Act I with **30 Bucks**.
- Earn: **+10 per 10,000 points**, `bucks` pickups, mission completion bonus (10–25), optional objectives (+5 each).
- **Before a mission and on each death**: spend Bucks to deploy a tank from the unlocked roster (costs in doc 03).
- **0 Bucks and no tank deployed = mission failed** → retry from mission start with the Bucks you entered with (not game over for the whole campaign — *Enhancement*, less punishing).
- Difficulty scales costs: Easy ×0.7, Normal ×1.0, Hard ×1.3.

## 4.3 Recruitment (Original flavor)
Destroying/rescuing in certain missions recruits an **AI wingman** tank (max 2). Wingmen follow the player (see doc 06 "Ally AI") and persist across missions until destroyed. Gives the "build up your squad" fantasy from the original.

## 4.4 Mission types
| Type | Objective | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Clear Zone** | Destroy all hostiles (wave-based) | Ruined city block |
| **Rescue** | Reach a captive QueenLord, carry her to extraction | Classic finale type |
| **Escort** | Protect convoy trucks to destination (Convoy mode in SP) | Supply run |
| **Defend** | Hold a fortress against waves for N minutes | Base siege |
| **Assault** | Destroy specific structures (generators, gates) | Break the blockade |
| **Pursuit** | Chase and destroy a fleeing target before it escapes | Convoy ambush |
| **Boss** | Fight a super-tank or fortress with phases | Act finales |
| **Survival** *(optional)* | Last N minutes vs escalating waves | Bonus challenges |

Each mission has: 1 primary objective, 0–2 optional objectives (no deaths, under par time, find cache), 3 hidden caches (Bucks or lore).

## 4.6 Bosses
Every boss: 3 phases at 66% / 33% HP; each phase changes attack pattern and summons adds. Telegraphed attacks (ground markers 1 s before impact). Weak points (rear vents) take ×2.

## 4.7 Co-op campaign (from Global Assault)
- 2 players (local split-screen or online); **Enhancement**: allow up to 4 online.
- **Shared Bucks** pool (Original). A downed player can redeploy only if the pool can afford it.
- Enemy count ×1.5 (2p), ×2.0 (3–4p); boss HP ×1.6 per extra player.
- Drop-in mid-mission at next checkpoint.

## 4.8 Scoring
Points: kill light 500, medium 750, heavy 1000, structure 50–300, objective 2500, no-death bonus 5000, time bonus up to 5000. End-of-mission grade (S/A/B/C) feeds into progression XP (doc 07).

## 4.9 Story delivery
Cheap and web-friendly: illustrated stills + text + optional voice, 30–60 s between missions. In-mission radio barks for context. All skippable.
