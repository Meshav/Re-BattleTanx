# 04 — Single-Player Campaign

> **Status (Oct 2026, S32):** both acts (24 missions) are built, and every mission is tested for marker sanity, drivability and scripted completion. Mission data: `data/campaign/act1.json` and `act2.json` (Act II is generated by `scripts/build-act2.py`); numbers in `data/campaign/rules.json`. On screen, Tank Bucks are **Scrap** and the Edge is **the Rift** (`data/names.json`). ⏳ marks what isn't built.

## 4.1 Structure
Two acts mirroring the two original games, written with original characters.

- **Act I — "The Long Road West"** ✅ (BattleTanx homage): 12 missions, coast-to-coast US. Goal: rescue the protagonist's partner from a rival QueenLord.
- **Act II — "The Long Road East"** ✅ (Global Assault homage): 12 missions, US → Europe. Goal: stop an antagonist QueenLord hunting the protagonists' Edge-gifted child.
- **Secret missions** ⏳: 1 per act, unlocked by finding all hidden caches in that act.

~24 missions × 5–10 min ≈ 3–4 hours. Longer and more varied than the originals (a common criticism).

## 4.2 Economy: Tank Bucks (from Global Assault)
- Start Act I with **30 Bucks**.
- Earn: **+10 per 10,000 points**, `bucks` pickups, mission completion bonus (10–25), optional objectives (+5 each).
- **Before a mission and on each death**: spend Bucks to deploy a tank from the unlocked roster (costs in doc 03).
- **0 Bucks and no tank deployed = mission failed** → retry from mission start with the Bucks you entered with (not game over for the whole campaign — *Enhancement*, less punishing). ✅ As built: progress (wallet, unlocked missions, best grades) is saved in the browser only when a mission is **won**, so a failed mission costs nothing.
- Difficulty scales costs: Easy ×0.7, Normal ×1.0, Hard ×1.3. ✅

## 4.3 Recruitment (Original flavor)
Destroying/rescuing in certain missions recruits an **AI wingman** tank. Wingmen follow the player (doc 06 §6.8) and give the "build up your squad" fantasy from the original.
✅ As built: each mission lists its own wingmen (`allies`); from Midwest Rally on, the freed driver rides along as an extra Torch. ⏳ Wingmen persisting across missions until destroyed.

## 4.4 Mission types
| Type | Objective | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Clear Zone** | Destroy all hostiles (wave-based) | Ruined city block |
| **Rescue** | Reach a captive QueenLord, carry her to extraction | Classic finale type |
| **Escort** | Protect convoy trucks to destination (Convoy mode in SP) | Supply run |
| **Defend** | Hold a fortress against waves for N minutes | Base siege |
| **Assault** | Destroy specific structures (generators, gates) | Break the blockade |
| **Pursuit** | Chase and destroy a fleeing target before it escapes | Convoy ambush |
| **Boss** | Fight a super-tank or fortress with phases | Act finales |
| **Survival** *(optional)* | Last N minutes vs escalating waves | Bonus challenges |

All types are built ✅ (Survival is the `survive` objective, e.g. holding the Pennsylvania Turnpike for 90 s). Objective kinds in data: `destroyAll`, `destroyTargets`, `reach`, `rescue`, `survive`, `stopRunners`, `destroyTurrets`, plus bonus `noDeaths` / `parTime` / `caches`. Waves trigger at start, after N seconds, when a wave is cleared or when a point is reached.

Each mission has: 1 primary objective, 0–2 optional objectives (no deaths, under par time, find caches, +5 Scrap each), hidden caches under `C` buildings (Scrap). ⏳ Lore caches.

## 4.6 Bosses ✅
The **Warlord** (900 HP, rear hits ×2) drives every boss fight: 3 phases at 66% / 33% HP; each phase changes the barrage pattern and summons adds (two Scouts, then two Rangers). Telegraphed artillery strikes (red ground rings 1 s before impact, 35 dmg in 5 m). A shockwave when you get within 12 m from phase 2 on; enraged (permanent boost) in phase 3. Killing it is worth 5000 points.

## 4.7 Co-op campaign (from Global Assault)
- ✅ 2 players, local split-screen, **shared Scrap** pool (Original). A downed player can redeploy only if the pool can afford it; the deploy screen auto-picks after 8 s for pad players.
- ⏳ Online co-op, up to 4 players, enemy count ×1.5 (2p) / ×2.0 (3–4p) and boss HP ×1.6 per extra player, drop-in at checkpoints. Today the enemy count doesn't scale with players.

## 4.8 Scoring ✅
Points: kill light 500, medium 750, heavy 1000, structure 100, target 600, objective 2500, boss 5000, no-death bonus 5000, time bonus up to 5000 (linear, zero at twice par time). **+10 Scrap per 10,000 points.** Grade: S ≥ 16,000 · A ≥ 11,000 · B ≥ 7,000 · else C. The grade feeds progression XP (doc 07 §7.2).

## 4.9 Story delivery
